# SAHAAYALEARN — CHILD DATA PROTECTION & DPDP ACT 2023 COMPLIANCE

## 1. Statutory Mandate & Scope

The Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 sets stringent regulations for processing personal data belonging to children (individuals under the age of 18) under **Section 9**.

SahaayaLearn is engineered from the ground up to be an educational safe haven for minors (specifically target Classes 6–10, ages 11–16).

---

## 2. Core Pillars of Child Safety & Privacy

### 2.1 Verifiable Parental / Guardian Consent (Section 9(1))
- **Consent Gateway:** Before any personalized profiling or telemetry is enabled, a parent or legal guardian must link their mobile number and provide verifiable consent via OTP.
- **Granular Consent:** Parents can selectively grant consent for:
  - Academic progress tracking and mastery analytics.
  - Spoken audio processing for oral tutoring.
  - Periodic WhatsApp/SMS audio summaries (Family Voice Bridge).
- **Consent Revocation:** Parents can revoke consent or request complete erasure at any time via the Parent Portal (`/guardian/dashboard`).

### 2.2 Ban on Behavioral Tracking & Targeted Profiling (Section 9(2))
- SahaayaLearn **never** conducts behavioral monitoring, ad-targeting, or commercial profiling of children.
- Telemetry is strictly restricted to educational diagnostics: identifying misconceptions (e.g., mistaking denominators in fraction operations) and calibrating spaced repetition intervals (SM-2 algorithm).
- No third-party ad networks, tracking pixels, or commercial telemetry SDKs exist in the codebase.

### 2.3 Prohibition of Detrimental Processing (Section 9(3))
- The platform enforces an absolute ban on processing child data that could cause physical, mental, or emotional harm.
- **Sahaaya AI Guardrails:** The AI Tutor refuses to engage in negative self-evaluation, cyberbullying, or harmful topics, redirecting the student gently to verified curriculum or suggesting consultation with a trusted teacher or guardian.

### 2.4 Zero Voiceprint Retention
- Spoken audio streams sent to the Sarvam / speech recognition service are treated as ephemeral audio buffers.
- Audio packets are processed in real-time memory and discarded immediately once text transcription is obtained.
- **No biometrics, acoustic fingerprints, or voice models of children are ever stored or trained upon.**

---

## 3. Account Security & Privacy Architecture

### 3.1 Pseudonymous Learner IDs
- Students are identified primarily by randomized, non-reversible Learner IDs (e.g. `SL-8K4P92`).
- Students in rural or semi-urban schools can log in with their Learner ID and password without requiring personal email addresses or phone numbers.
- Learner IDs prevent the exposure of full legal names in API parameters and school-wide dashboards.

### 3.2 Student-Parent Boundary & Safe Space
- While parents have full oversight over academic outcomes (topics mastered, study hours, upcoming exams), **direct student-tutor conversational transcripts are kept private**.
- This pedagogical design preserves a psychologically safe learning environment where first-generation learners feel comfortable asking "basic" questions without anxiety or fear of reprimand.
- Parents receive synthetic, high-level narrative summaries (Family Voice Bridge) generated by AI to bridge the understanding gap.

### 3.3 Data Minimization & Retention Schedule
| Data Category | Retention Limit | Encryption Standard | Purge Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| Raw Spoken Audio | Ephemeral (0 seconds) | TLS 1.3 in-transit | Discarded after transcription |
| AI Tutor Queries | 30 days rolling | AES-256 at rest | Automatic TTL cascade |
| Academic Twin Mastery | Active academic year | AES-256 + Tenant RLS | Exportable on guardian request |
| Parent Consent Records | 3 years statutory | Cryptographic audit log | Tamper-proof hash ledger |

## 4. Emergency Credential Recovery

For rural and first-generation families who may lose passwords or forget login credentials:
1. The Parent/Guardian logs into the Parent Portal using their registered phone number.
2. The guardian selects **"Recover Child Credentials"**.
3. A secure one-time reset code is verified, instantly restoring access to the student's Learner ID without requiring email access.
